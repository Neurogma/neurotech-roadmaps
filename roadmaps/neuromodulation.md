# I want to understand neuromodulation

Learners who want a scientifically grounded map of nervous-system modulation methods and the evidence around them.

## Destination

- Differentiate recording, decoding and modulation at the system level.
- Compare broad neuromodulation classes by mechanism and evidence type.
- Critically appraise claims without turning the learning path into a stimulation protocol.

## Starting assumptions

- Start with physiology and evidence appraisal, not device operation.

## Common misconceptions

- Mechanistic plausibility is not evidence of clinical efficacy.
- Parameters cannot be interpreted independently of device, protocol, tissue and indication context.

## Typical failure modes

- Extrapolating from small studies and omitting safety, evidence quality or jurisdictional context.

## Research directions

- Closed-loop modulation, individualization, biomarkers, mechanism-informed models and long-term outcomes.

## Safety

The repository does not provide stimulation parameters or invasive procedural instructions. Real stimulation belongs in appropriate professional settings.

## Ethics

Consent, risk, access, evidence quality and long-term responsibility are central to evaluation.
